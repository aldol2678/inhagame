## Asset (`assets/build_equipment.py`)

*Public note:* the builder is kept in the private repository; see `EQUIPMENT_ASSET_BINDING_P0.md`.

Generated by the same deterministic builder as the cap and the backpack (rebuilding reproduces all
three GLBs byte for byte; a test checks it). Authored directly in **TOP-anchor space** (the anchor is
0.55 × height above the feet on the body axis), from the Induck GLB body numbers
(`round_white_body` centre (0, -0.31, 0), radii (0.83, 0.77, 0.68), scaled to HUMAN_HEIGHT):

- **`hoodie_body`** — one seamless shell following the body ellipsoid **0.008 outside it** (no
  z-fighting, no visible puffing), from the hips up to the shoulders (it stops where the body is
  still wider than the neck, so it never passes through the neck). One grid, analytic ellipsoid
  normals: no seams between pieces.
- **`hoodie_hem`** — orange band at the hem (matches the cap band and the backpack flap).
- **`hoodie_collar`** — a short dark cone from the shoulder opening in to the neck.
- **`hoodie_hood`** — a soft hood volume resting behind the neck, well below the head: it never
  covers the head and stays clear of the cap and the backpack.
- **`hoodie_pocket`** — kangaroo pocket on the front, `hoodie_logo_I` a white "I" chest mark,
  `hoodie_drawstring_L/R` white drawstrings.
- **No sleeves.** The wings flap; a sleeve on a fixed anchor would swallow them.

### Wings

The Induck base GLB has no jacket geometry (only the primitive fallback does); its wings are small
ellipsoids whose pivot sits inside the body. Their visible part is the part that sticks out past the
body, which also sticks out past a 0.008 shell, so the wings come out through the hoodie like arms
through a sleeveless opening. A cut-out opening was tried and dropped: a grid-cut hole only showed
jagged white body around the wing. During the wave emote the base character itself tucks the waving
wing into its body; the hoodie does not change that.

## Fit and co-wearing

- Cap + hoodie + backpack are three distinct silhouettes (head / body / back). The hood sits above
  the backpack's top and behind the neck; the backpack's front face rests against the hoodie shell.
- Fit follows every pose through the existing anchors (idle, walk, run, jump, sit pitch, emote,
  rotation, dragon, bike); the sit / bike pivot fix from Asset Binding P0 keeps the shell on the body.
- First person hides it with the character, like the other equipment.
