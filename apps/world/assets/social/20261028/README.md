# INHA WORLD actual-gameplay social images

These files replace the withdrawn concept-art candidate. They use the user's
`1000014898.jpg` gameplay capture: a flying character beside the campus dragon
monument. The original is preserved in the review ZIP, with its SHA-256 in
`manifest.json`. Exact capture date/build and production/local origin were not
independently verified; 2026-10-28 is the date received from the user.

Adobe removed phone system bars by cropping, extracted the central scene and
resized each aspect ratio. Characters, buildings, lighting and the operator/GM
nameplate were not generated, retouched or replaced. Peripheral game controls
fall outside the crops; this is not a demonstration of a UI-free game mode.
No title or other new text was painted over the screenshot.

The source scene is 910 × 570. Exact source crops and effective scaling are
recorded per export. Portrait first exported at 912 × 1140 due to Adobe's resize
cap, then received a resize-only pass to 1080 × 1350. Enlarging does not add detail.

All outputs were visually inspected and decoded to verify dimensions, opaque RGB
pixels and hashes. Landscape is below 500 KB; companions are below 1 MB. Public
metadata declares image/png, 1200 × 630 and summary_large_image.

This is a local release candidate, not a deployed or externally cached result.
